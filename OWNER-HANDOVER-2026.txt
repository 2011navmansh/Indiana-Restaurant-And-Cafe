 7) MENU ITEMS + PRICES
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
File: menu-data.js  (yahi poora menu hai)

Ek line ka format:
   {"n":"Paneer Jhalawari","d":"Cottage cheese with 3 types of Rajasthani hot chillies.","p":250,"img":"https://...","t":"spicy2"},

   n = dish ka naam
   d = description
   p = PRICE (number, sirf digits - Rs nahi likhna)
   img = dish ki photo ka link
   t = tag ("" = normal, "spicy", "chef", "star" waghaira)

Naya dish jodna hai? Us jaisi line copy karke neeche paste kar do aur
comma (,) lagana na bhoolo.
Price badalni hai? Sirf "p": ke aage ka number badlo: "p":250 -> "p":280

ZAROORI: abhi dish photos internet (Wikimedia) se li gayi hain, wo
asli dish jaisi nahi hongi. Behtar hoga ki apne restaurant ki asli
dish photos postimg.cc pe daal ke link badal do.

  1. Admin -> CSV button -> bookings ka file download (mahine me ek baar)
